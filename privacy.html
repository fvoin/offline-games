Privacy Policy

Om Nom Offline · last updated: 13 October 2026

Om Nom Offline is a collection of mini-games that works without an internet connection. The short version: the game does not collect any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. It does not ask for your name, e-mail address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and it does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress (candies, stars, costumes, unlocked games, your drawings, and the sound, music and vibration settings) is stored only on your device, in the storage of the app or of your browser. It never leaves the device. If you clear the storage of the app or the browser, or uninstall the game, this data is deleted.

Vibration

The game can make your device vibrate for feedback during play. Vibration is used only if it is switched on in the game settings, and you can switch it off at any time.

Internet connection

After it is installed (or, for the web version, after it has been opened once), the game works fully offline. The web version downloads its own files from the site it is opened from, and keeps them in your browser so that it can start without a connection. No other connections are made.

Children

The game is suitable for the whole family, including children. Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing to collect from children either. Links that lead outside the game are placed behind a grown-up check.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
